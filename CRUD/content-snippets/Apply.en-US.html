How to Apply

1

Review the eligibility criteria to verify if your organization and proposed private forest land meets the criteria to apply.

Refer to the application resources for additional information, or contact your regional program manager for assistance.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicants must be from local governmental entities, federally recognized Indian Tribes (including Alaska Native Corporations), and qualified conservation nonprofit organizations that are qualified to acquire and manage land.
  • Applicants must be from local governmental entities, federally recognized Indian Tribes (including Alaska Native Corporations), and qualified conservation nonprofit organizations that are qualified to acquire and manage land.
  • Private forest land must be at least five acres in size.
  • Private forest land must be suitable to sustain natural vegetation.
  • Private forest land must be at least 75 percent forested.
  • Private forest land must be threatened by conversion to non-forest uses.
  • Private forest land must not be held in trust by the United States on behalf of any Indian Tribe and not Tribal allotment lands.
  • Private forest land must be offered for sale by a willing seller.
  • Private forest land must be able to have public access.
